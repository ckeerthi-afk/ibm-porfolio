Section: The Challenge
Heading
From Enterprise Complexity to SaaS Simplicity
Body
IBM Maximo has long been a powerful enterprise asset management platform used by large organizations. However, its depth and flexibility often came with complexity. New users faced steep learning curves, dense interfaces, and lengthy onboarding processes before experiencing value.
The Small SaaS initiative aimed to reimagine Maximo for mid-sized businesses by creating a guided, visual-first experience that reduced complexity without sacrificing capability.
As a UX Design Intern on a cross-functional team of designers, engineers, and product managers, I contributed to research, onboarding strategy, workflow simplification, and visual design initiatives that helped make the platform more approachable for first-time users.

Section: Discovery & Research
Heading
Understanding What Great SaaS Onboarding Looks Like
Body
Before designing solutions, I investigated how successful SaaS products help users reach value quickly.
I conducted competitive research focused on onboarding experiences, studying how users moved from account creation to their first successful outcome.
The primary benchmark was MaintainX, a modern maintenance management platform often praised for its intuitive onboarding and ease of use.

MaintainX
